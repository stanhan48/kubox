Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Kubox website and Kubox Publisher, a private tool that publishes Kubox videos to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

Who uses the app

Kubox Publisher is used only by the Kubox team to manage the Kubox accounts. It is not available to the public and does not collect data from viewers or other users.

What data the app accesses

  • Access tokens for the connected Kubox accounts on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube (Google), granted through each platform's official login.
  • Basic account information of those accounts (account ID, username, channel name) to confirm the right account is connected.
  • The videos, captions and titles we create and choose to publish.

How the data is used

Only to upload and publish our own videos to our own accounts and to read back the link of the published post. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only locally on the team's own computer. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (Meta/Instagram, TikTok, Google/YouTube) through their official APIs.

Google user data

Kubox Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions), in TikTok's security settings, or in Instagram's apps and websites settings. Deleting the local token files removes all stored data. Requests can be sent to the contact below.

This website

This website has no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
